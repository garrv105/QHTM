# Code of Conduct

QHTM adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, as its code of conduct.

## Our standards

We are building shared, trustworthy infrastructure for an emerging field. That requires a community where people can contribute, disagree, and correct one another in good faith. We commit to:

- **Professional, respectful engagement** — critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- **Evidence over assertion** — claims about techniques and their maturity are backed by citations or demonstrations.
- **Honesty about uncertainty** — it is always acceptable, and encouraged, to say "this is theoretical" or "I'm not sure."
- **Good-faith disclosure** — contributors with commercial or institutional interests disclose them.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks**, in any project space.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public space where an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer by opening a confidential issue or contacting the Founding Editor via the repository. Reports are reviewed promptly and in confidence. The maintainer may take any action deemed appropriate, from a warning to removal from project spaces.

For the full text and enforcement guidelines, see the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
